## Where they stood

This client was a hypnotherapist whose work spoke for itself — clients trusted them the moment they tried a session. The problem was marketing. They'd run ads, tried every tactic, but bookings stayed unpredictable — "just riding the wave," as they described it.

## What I did

Instead of pushing for a bigger ad spend, I first identified the real psychological barrier: the word "hypnosis" itself made a lot of prospects hesitant, and they needed more trust before taking the first step.

I designed messaging specifically for that audience and built an automated follow-up flow — every inquiry got an instant response, so nobody felt ignored, without the practitioner having to chase anyone personally.

## What this case proves

Being great at your craft doesn't automatically fill your calendar. **What clients need is consistent, caring follow-up** — not a bigger ad budget. Automation freed up time to do the work they're actually best at.
