## Where they stood

This EdTech client came to me facing a squeeze from bigger, better-funded competitors — ad costs kept climbing, inquiries kept coming in, but few ever converted. As the owner put it: "We don't lack traffic. We lack people who actually follow through and sign up."

## What I did

I didn't tell them to spend more on ads. First, I ran a three-sided analysis — what excited prospects, what worried them, and what was neutral. It turned out parents weren't reluctant to enroll — they just weren't confident their kids would actually improve.

So I designed a free "skills assessment" as the first touchpoint, backed by instant WhatsApp follow-up and CRM reminders so nothing fell through the cracks. Real student success stories (not actors) went out on social media, giving parents their own reasons to trust the process.

![Project Image](/images/step-by-step.jpg)

## Results

- **332% revenue growth** in 4 months
- Conversion rate up nearly 50%
- Team response time 60% faster — without adding headcount

## In their words

*"We used to be the ones chasing every inquiry. Now parents reach out to us first."*

---

## What this case proves

Traffic was never the real bottleneck — **confidence was**. Give people a low-risk first step and they'll come to you; you won't have to chase them.
